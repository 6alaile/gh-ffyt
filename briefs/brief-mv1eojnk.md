# 🎬 Content Brief: Yanga 18, Simba 18 — The Ligi Kuu Table Before the Derby

## The Idea

**Core concept:** After six matchdays of the 2026/27 NBC Premier League, Young Africans and Simba SC are level on 18 points, six wins from six, and they meet in the first Kariakoo Derby of the season on Saturday 10 October.
**Unique angle:** Not a standings read-out. A "table before the collision": who is really chasing, what the derby does to the table whichever way it lands, and why the CAF second-round trips a week later make this the most loaded fortnight of the Ligi Kuu season.
**Why now:** The derby is Saturday 10 October, 7pm EAT, at the renovated Uhuru Stadium. The league has been on hold since mid-September for the FIFA break, so search intent peaks on derby eve, and the CAF second-round first legs follow on 16–18 October.

## Audience

**Primary viewer:** A 20–30 year old Tanzanian football fan in Dar es Salaam or the diaspora, who follows Simba or Yanga closely, argues about the derby daily, but does not track the rest of the table.
**What they're feeling before they click:** Derby-eve tension and banter. They know both teams are flying but want the actual numbers.
**What they should feel after watching:** Informed and ready. They know exactly where every contender stands and what each derby result would do to the table.
