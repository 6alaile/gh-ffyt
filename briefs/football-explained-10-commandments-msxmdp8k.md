## Scene 2 — Law 1: The Field of Play

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 1
**Headline:** The Field of Play
**Items:**
- Pitch length: 90–120 metres
- Pitch width: 45–90 metres
- Centre circle radius: 9.15 metres
- Goal size: 7.32m wide, 2.44m high

**Query:** aerial view football pitch markings

> "Law 1 sets the stage — literally. Every line on the pitch has an exact measurement."

## Scene 3 — Law 2: The Ball

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 2
**Headline:** The Ball
**Items:**
- Circumference: 68–70cm
- Weight: 410–450 grams at kickoff
- Must be spherical, made of suitable material
- Referee approves it before every match

**Query:** close up football rolling on grass

> "Law 2 is short but strict — even the ball itself has to meet exact specs."

## Scene 4 — Law 3: The Players

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 3
**Headline:** The Players
**Items:**
- Max 11 players per team, one must be goalkeeper
- Minimum 7 players needed to start a match
- Up to 5 substitutes allowed in most competitions
- Sent-off players cannot be replaced

**Query:** football team lineup walking out tunnel

> "Law 3 decides who's even allowed on the pitch."

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 4
**Headline:** Players' Equipment
**Items:**
- Jersey, shorts, socks, shin guards, footwear required
- Shin guards must be covered by socks
- No jewelry allowed, for player safety
- Goalkeepers wear colors distinct from both teams

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 5
**Headline:** The Referee

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 7
**Headline:** Duration of the Match
**Items:**
- Two halves of 45 minutes each
- Half-time break of no more than 15 minutes
- Stoppage time added for delays
- Extra time only in knockout competitions

> "Law 7 sets the clock — though stoppage time always adds a twist."

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 8
**Headline:** Start & Restart of Play
**Items:**
- Kick-off starts each half and after every goal
- Coin toss decides ends and first kick-off
- Dropped ball restarts play after stoppages
- Ball must move forward to be in play

**Kind:** list
**Duration:** 22s
**Eyebrow:** LAW 9
**Headline:** Ball In & Out of Play
**Items:**
- Out of play when it fully crosses a line
- Stays in play if it rebounds off officials or posts
- Play continues until the referee stops it
- Goal line technology confirms the tight calls

**Kind:** list

**Headline:** Determining the Outcome
**Items:**

- Equal goals means a draw, unless competition rules say otherwise
- Knockouts may need extra time or penalties
- Goals only count if fully over the line
